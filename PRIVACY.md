# Privacy Policy — Rust & Riches

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

Rust & Riches ("the game") is developed by haiider820. This policy explains
what data the game collects and why.

## Data we collect

**Account & gameplay data** (via Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore)
- An anonymous account ID (or your Google account ID if you link one).
- Gameplay state: cash, cars, upgrades, mission progress, purchase history.
- This data is required for the game to function and sync across reinstalls.

**Crash & performance data** (via Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics)
- Crash reports and anonymized usage events (e.g. "car sold") used to fix
  bugs and improve the game. No advertising identifiers are collected by us.

**Ads** (via Google AdMob)
- AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve ads. On
  first launch in regions where consent is required (EEA/UK), the game shows
  Google's consent form (UMP) before any ad loads. You can change your
  choice anytime from the privacy options.

**Purchases** (via RevenueCat / Google Play / App Store)
- Purchase receipts are validated to unlock the VIP Garage Pass and
  consumables. Payment details are handled by the store, never by us.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not collect precise location, contacts, or photos.

## Your rights

You may request deletion of your gameplay data by deleting the app and
contacting the developer. Deleting the app removes local data; server data
tied to an anonymous ID cannot be re-identified after the ID is lost.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the GitHub repository.
